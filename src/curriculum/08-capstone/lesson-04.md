# --instructions--

The goal of this course was to give you a foundation to build upon, and to give you the confidence to explore Vim on your own.

If you'd like to keep learning, here are some next steps:

- Practice using Vim in your daily workflow.
- Explore Vim’s built-in help system on your machine with `:help`.
- Learn advanced editing concepts like text objects, macros, and registers.
- Learn about Vim plugins and how to customize your Vim environment.
- Use [Vim documentation](https://www.vim.org/docs.php) for more in-depth information.
