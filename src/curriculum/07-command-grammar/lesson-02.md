# --author-notes--

## CAG

**Concept:**
Text objects describe "what," independent of cursor position; `iw` means "the word the cursor is in," usable with `d` or `c` (`diw`, `ciw`).

**Activity:**
Delete or change a word using `diw`/`ciw` without first moving to its start.

**Goal:**
The learner edits a word from anywhere inside it.
